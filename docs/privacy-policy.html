Privacy Policy for ExaMore

Effective Date: November 26, 2026  |  Developer: Rishvi  |  Application: ExaMore (com.examore.app)

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to ExaMore ("we", "our", or "us"), developed and maintained by Rishvi. We provide an advanced educational and examination platform designed to facilitate secure assessments, smart classroom cohort management, and comprehensive candidate performance analytics.

We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data transparently. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored and secured, and your rights regarding account and data deletion in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Google Play Data Safety guidelines.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal and diagnostic data required to provide a reliable, fair, and cheat-resistant assessment environment:

Data Category Specific Data Collected Primary Purpose
Personal Account Data Full Name, Email Address, 10-digit Mobile Number, Profile Photo URL, User Role (Student / Admin). Account registration, authentication, student identification on scorecards, and instructor linking.
Academic & Assessment Data Classroom enrollments, exam responses, scores, accuracy percentage, time taken, attempt counts. Grading exams, generating performance analytics, enforcing allowed attempt limits, and gradebook export.
Anti-Cheating & Integrity Data Screen/app switch count during an active exam, window blur events. Upholding academic integrity and preventing unauthorized multitasking during live timed tests.
Device & Notification Data Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Device Push Tokens, network connectivity state. Delivering real push notifications for new exams, deadline updates, and 1-hour closing alerts.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected data strictly for educational and service delivery purposes, including:

  • Authenticating candidates and instructors securely via email/password or Google Sign-In.
  • Enforcing examination timeslot schedules (Publish/Open windows and Close/Deadline limits).
  • Delivering push and local system tray notifications for scheduled assessments.
  • Caching exam progress locally on the device (using secure storage) so students do not lose progress during unexpected network interruptions.
  • Providing instructors with aggregated analytics and exportable CSV reports for institutional evaluation.

Zero Commercial Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or assessment information to advertisers or commercial third-party brokers under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver cloud synchronization and enterprise-grade reliability, ExaMore integrates the following certified third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: Manages secure user login, password hashing, and session tokens.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Hosts user profiles, classroom rosters, question banks, and examination submissions with encrypted cloud storage.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Sends automated push notifications for assessment schedules and deadline reminders.
  • Google Sign-In Services: Provides optional, one-tap identity verification using Google accounts.

5. Data Retention & Security Standards

All communications between the ExaMore client application and Firebase cloud services are strictly encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL). Stored records are protected using Firebase at-rest encryption standards.

Assessment results and student submission records are retained only for the duration required by the student's designated educational institution or until the user requests account deletion.

6. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, users have complete control over their personal data and can request permanent deletion at any time:

Option A: In-App Direct Account Deletion

  1. Open the ExaMore app.
  2. Navigate to Profile > Settings > Account Management.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm your request.
  4. All personal profile records, bookmarks, examination attempts, and credentials will be permanently erased from our Firebase database and Authentication servers.

Option B: Web / Email Data Erasure Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can email our Data Protection team at:
📧 support@rishvi.co.uk with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - ExaMore" specifying your registered email address. Requests are processed within 48 to 72 business hours.

7. Children's Privacy

ExaMore is designed as an educational tool for schools, tutoring centers, and individual learners. When used by students under the age of 13, access is typically granted under the supervision of a school, educational institution, or parent/guardian in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors without educational cohort authorization.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in application features or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage users to review this page periodically.
