Student (Candidate) Guide

How students enroll in classrooms, take scheduled exams, and view performance breakdowns.

🔐

1. Registration & Sign-In

Sign up with your Name, Email, and 10-digit mobile number, or use Google Sign-In with one tap. Verify your email to activate assessment features.

🏫

2. Join Classrooms

Enter your instructor's 6-character Classroom Code on the Home screen to enroll in cohorts and unlock teacher-curated mock tests and study materials.

⏱️

3. Timed Assessments

Take scheduled exams within valid active windows. Progress auto-saves locally so you can resume safely if network connectivity drops.

1

Active Window & Timeslot Adherence

Exams are governed by an Open / Publish time and a Close / Deadline time. You can only start an exam while the window is active. Push notifications notify you when an exam opens or approaches its 1-hour closing deadline.

2

Anti-Cheating & Integrity Guard

During an active exam, switching away from the application or minimizing the window is recorded. Reaching the maximum allowed app switch threshold automatically submits your exam to uphold academic integrity.

3

Results, Answer Keys & Performance History

Upon submission, view your total marks, accuracy percentage, correct/incorrect questions, and passing status. Filter by subject to track performance improvement over time.

Instructor & Administrator Guide

Manage subjects, build comprehensive question banks, configure deadlines, and inspect candidate scorecards.

📚

Classrooms & Cohorts

Generate unique classroom codes, invite students, and organize subjects (Mathematics, Science, History, Literature) with isolated roster management.

📝

Exam Creator & Timers

Configure exam duration, total marks, negative marking, passing score percentages, and schedule exact start/end deadlines or publish instantly.

📊

Question Banks & CSV Import

Create single-choice, multiple-choice, or descriptive questions, or bulk-import hundreds of questions using our standardized CSV / Excel templates.

1

Independent Configurations & Question Editing

From the Exam card menu (⋮), instructors can choose to "Edit Configurations" (timings, duration, passing percentage) or "Edit Questions" independently without stepping through lengthy multi-step wizards.

2

Real-time Candidate Analytics & CSV Export

Inspect submissions in real time, view candidate-by-candidate answer breakdowns, and export complete gradebooks to CSV for institutional records.
